# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-20)

First release.

- `cook` skill: decode → design → verify → deliver workflow, with chef, nutritionist, meal-plan, label, fridge and rescue modes.
- Slash commands: `/chef`, `/nutritionist`, `/meal-plan`, `/nutrition-label`, `/fridge`, `/rescue`.
- Nutrition engine: USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy (6,781 foods, 42 nutrients), USDA portion weights, 650 ingredient aliases, as-cooked values from the USDA Table of Nutrient Retention Factors (Release 6) and USDA-derived yields, fat drained from rendered meats, flagged gap-filling, FDA-defined added sugars, hidden-allergen detection, live FoodData Central lookups for brands, personal targets, meal-plan totals and grocery lists.
- Reference library: flavor science, kitchen chemistry and food safety, numbers, cravings, substitutions, nutrition, meal planning, baking, rescue.
