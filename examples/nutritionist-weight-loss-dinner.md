## Sheet-Pan Chicken Shawarma Plate

Shawarma-spiced chicken thighs roasted until the edges char, with a pile of roasted cauliflower and onions, cold garlicky yogurt, a crunchy cucumber-tomato salad, warm pita, and a spoonful of sizzling chili oil on top. Each plate is large and filling, with 61 g of protein and 670 calories.

**Serves** 4 · **Active** 25 min · **Total** 1 hr (including 15 min marinating) · **Equipment** 2 rimmed sheet pans, foil

Each serving is one dinner plate, so you get 4 dinners or lunches. Leftovers keep 4 days in the fridge.

**What I aimed for, based on your stats:** about 1,900 calories a day (a deficit of roughly 470 below what you burn) and about 150 g protein a day. This dinner is 670 calories with 61 g protein, so you have about 1,230 calories left for the rest of the day. Protein and fiber (11 g here) are what keep you full.

### Ingredients
**For the chicken**
- 800 g (1¾ lb) boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 100 g (⅓ cup + 1 tbsp) nonfat plain Greek yogurt
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) lemon juice
- 4 garlic cloves, grated
- 2 tsp ground cumin, 2 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp ground coriander, ½ tsp turmeric, ½ tsp cinnamon
- 15 g (1 tbsp) olive oil
- 5 g salt (1¾ tsp Diamond Crystal kosher · 1 tsp Morton kosher · ¾ tsp table salt)

**For the vegetables**
- 1 large head cauliflower (about 700 g), cut into florets
- 2 medium red onions, cut into 8 wedges each
- 10 g (2 tsp) olive oil
- 2 g salt (¾ tsp Diamond Crystal · ½ tsp Morton · ⅓ tsp table salt)

**For the garlic-mint yogurt**
- 250 g (1 cup) nonfat plain Greek yogurt
- 2 garlic cloves, grated
- Zest of 1 lemon plus 15 ml (1 tbsp) of its juice
- 10 g mint leaves (a small handful), chopped. Dill works too.
- 1 g salt (⅓ tsp Diamond Crystal · ¼ tsp Morton · a big pinch of table salt)

**For the salad and finish**
- 1 English cucumber, diced
- 300 g (2 cups) cherry tomatoes, halved
- 20 g parsley (a big handful), chopped
- 1 g salt (same as for the yogurt)
- 4 whole-wheat pitas
- 15 g (1 tbsp) olive oil
- 1 tsp red pepper flakes (Aleppo pepper is better if you can find it)

### Method
**Game plan:** marinate the chicken first, then make the yogurt and salad while the oven heats and the pans roast.

1. **Marinate.** Whisk the yogurt, lemon juice, garlic, spices, oil and salt in a bowl. Set aside 1 tbsp of this mix for the cauliflower, then toss the thighs in the rest. Leave at least 15 minutes, or cover and refrigerate up to overnight. The yogurt's acid and calcium tenderize the meat and help it brown.
2. **Heat the oven** to 450°F (230°C) with racks in the upper and lower thirds. Line both pans with foil.
3. **Build the pans.** Lay the chicken flat on one pan with space between pieces. Crowded chicken steams and won't char. On the second pan, toss the cauliflower and onions with the oil, salt and the 1 tbsp of marinade you set aside, then spread them in a single layer.
4. **Roast 20-25 min.** Put the chicken on the upper rack and the vegetables on the lower one. The chicken is done when the edges are browned and it reads at least 175°F (80°C) at the thickest point. Thighs stay juicy at that temperature, so don't pull them early. The cauliflower is done when the cut sides are deep golden.
5. **Char.** Move the chicken pan to the top rack and broil 2-3 min until the edges blacken in spots. Watch it, because it goes from charred to burnt in about a minute. Rest the chicken 5 min, then slice it thin. Warm the pitas directly on the oven rack for 1 minute.
6. **Make the yogurt and salad while it roasts.** Stir the yogurt ingredients together. In another bowl, toss the cucumber, tomatoes, parsley and salt, but only right before eating so the salad stays crisp.
7. **Make the chili oil and serve.** Heat the oil and pepper flakes in a small pan over medium until it sizzles and turns red, about 1 min. Plate the chicken, vegetables, salad and a big spoonful of yogurt, then spoon the hot chili oil over the yogurt.

### Why it works
- **The calories you can taste are kept on the surface.** The flavor comes from browning, char, spices, garlic and lemon, which cost almost nothing. Most of the oil goes into the chili oil on top, where you taste it first, so one tablespoon does the job of four.
- **Thighs instead of breast.** They are hard to dry out, they char well, and they taste much richer for about 40 extra calories per serving.
- **Hot and cold, crisp and creamy.** Charred meat, cold tangy yogurt, a crunchy salad and soft pita in each bite keep the last bite as good as the first.
- **It's a large volume of food.** Each plate weighs nearly 800 g (1¾ lb), and most of that is low-calorie vegetables. Eating more food for fewer calories is what makes a calorie deficit sustainable.

### Make it yours
- **Cut more calories:** skip the pita and serve it as a bowl (about 500 calories), or use half a pita.
- **Hungrier days:** add a 400 g can of chickpeas, drained and patted dry, to the vegetable pan for crunch, more fiber and about 90 more calories per serving.
- **Meal prep:** keep the chicken, vegetables, yogurt and salad in separate containers. Reheat only the chicken and vegetables.
