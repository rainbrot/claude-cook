## Salt targets

## Salt conversions

| Salt | g per tsp | g per tbsp | Sodium mg per g |
|---|---|---|---|
| Table salt, fine sea salt | 6 | 18 | 390 |
| Morton kosher | 4.8 | 14.4 | 390 |
| Diamond Crystal kosher | 2.8 | 8.4 | 390 |
| Flaky (Maldon), approx. | 2 | 6 | 390 |

Format for recipes: `7 g salt (2½ tsp Diamond Crystal · 1½ tsp Morton kosher · 1¼ tsp table)`. Salt in grams → Diamond tsp = g ÷ 2.8; Morton tsp = g ÷ 4.8; table tsp = g ÷ 6. Round to the nearest ¼ tsp.

| Parmesan | 22-33 g (¼-⅓ cup finely grated) | glutamate, fat, nuttiness |
| Anchovy fillets (in oil) | 10 g (2-3 fillets) | inosinate and glutamate |
| Feta | 35 g | tang |

## Acid and sugar

- Lemon juice is about 5-6% citric acid (pH ~2.2); lime is similar. One lemon gives about 45 ml (3 tbsp) juice; one lime about 30-45 ml.
- Vinegars: distilled and cider about 5% acetic acid; rice vinegar about 4-4.5% (seasoned rice vinegar contains sugar and salt); sherry vinegar 7%; balsamic about 6% with significant sugar.
- Finishing acid for a pot of soup or braise (4 servings): start with 1-2 tsp vinegar or 1 tbsp lemon juice, taste, repeat.
- Quick pickle brine: 1 part 5% vinegar : 1 part water, 2-3% salt by weight of the liquid, 0-10% sugar.
- Sugar in savory sauces: 0.5-2% of the weight balances acid or heat without tasting sweet.
- Ice-cream base: 15-20% total sugar by weight. Sorbet: 20-30% sugar (it freezes too hard below that).

## Leavening and yeast

- Baking powder (double-acting): 1-1¼ tsp (4.6-5.8 g) per 125 g (1 cup) flour. 1 tsp = 4.6 g.
- Baking soda: ¼ tsp (1.2 g) per 125 g flour when the recipe contains acid. ½ tsp neutralizes about 1 cup (240 ml) buttermilk or yogurt, or 1 tbsp lemon juice or vinegar. Too much tastes soapy and metallic.
- Swap: 1 tsp baking powder ≈ ¼ tsp baking soda + ½ tsp cream of tartar.
- Yeast: 1 packet = 7 g = 2¼ tsp. Instant and active dry are interchangeable 1:1 in most recipes (active dry is slightly slower; bloom it in warm water first). Fresh yeast = 2-2.5× the dry weight.
- Water for blooming yeast: 40-45°C (105-115°F). Above 60°C kills it.

- Dry pasta roughly doubles to 2.2-2.5× its weight when cooked.

## Yields

| Raw → cooked | Weight change |
|---|---|
| Meat and poultry, roasted or seared | lose 25-35% |
| Braised meat | lose 35-45% |
| Bacon | lose 60-70% |
| Onions, caramelized | lose 60-75% |
| Mushrooms, sautéed | lose 40-50% |
| Spinach and leafy greens | lose 70-90% of volume, ~10-20% weight when wilted |
| Roasted vegetables | lose 20-40% |
| Dry pasta, boiled | gain 120-150% (×2.2-2.5) |
| Rice, boiled/absorbed | gain 180-200% (×2.8-3) |

Use these to estimate `yield:` for the engine. Sum the raw weights, apply the losses, and add any liquid that remains in the dish.

## Portions

Per adult main course, roughly:
- Boneless meat, poultry, fish: 125-170 g (4-6 oz) raw; bone-in: 225-340 g.
- Dry pasta: 85-115 g as a main (with sauce), 55-70 g as a starter.
- Raw rice: 60-90 g. Dry legumes: 50-75 g.
- Vegetables: 150-250 g per person across sides.
- Salad greens: 30-50 g side, 75-100 g main.
- Soup: 300-400 ml as a main, 200-250 ml as a starter.
- Dessert: cakes 80-120 g slice; ice cream 100-130 g.
- Protein per meal for most adults: 25-40 g.

| 9 × 9 in (23 cm square) | 81 in² / 523 cm² |
| 9 × 13 in (23 × 33 cm) | 117 in² / 755 cm² |

| Half sheet 18 × 13 in | 234 in² / 1,510 cm² |
| Quarter sheet 13 × 9 in | 117 in² / 755 cm² |
| 9 × 5 in loaf | about 8 cups volume |

- Scale batters by pan area at the same depth; deeper batter needs a lower oven (by about 10°C) and a longer bake.
- Scaling a recipe up: most ingredients scale linearly. For large batches (3× or more), start salt, spices, chili and leaveners at about 75-85% and adjust to taste. Cooking time scales with thickness, not weight; more food in the same pan means crowding and steaming, so use more pans or batches.
- Halving eggs: beat one and use about 25 g.

## Conversions

- 1 tsp = 4.93 ml; 1 tbsp = 3 tsp = 14.8 ml; 1 US cup = 16 tbsp = 237 ml; 1 fl oz = 29.6 ml. Metric cup (UK, AU, NZ) = 250 ml. AU tablespoon = 20 ml.
- 1 oz = 28.35 g; 1 lb = 454 g; 1 kg = 2.2 lb.
- °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9.
- 1 stick butter = ½ cup = 113 g = 8 tbsp.
- 1 can coconut milk = 400 ml (13.5 fl oz). 1 standard can of tomatoes = 400 g (14.5 oz) or 794 g (28 oz).
